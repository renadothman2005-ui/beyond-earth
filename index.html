Our Solar System

Meet the eight planets in our solar system!

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun.

Venus

The hottest planet in our solar system.

Earth

Our home planet, where humans, animals and plants live.

Mars

Known as the Red Planet because of its color.

Jupiter

The largest planet in our solar system.

Saturn

Famous for its beautiful rings.

Uranus

A very cold planet that rotates on its side.

Neptune

The farthest planet from the Sun.

Amazing Space Facts!

☀️ The Sun

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

🌙 The Moon

The Moon moves away from Earth by about 3.8 cm every year.

⭐ Shooting Stars

Shooting stars are actually small space rocks burning in our atmosphere.

🌌 Galaxies

Our galaxy is called the Milky Way.
